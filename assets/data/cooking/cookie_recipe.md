# 🍪 Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

> **Prep time:** 15 mins | **Bake time:** 10 mins | **Yields:** 24 cookies

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| All-Purpose Flour | 2 1/4 cups | Sifted |
| Baking Soda | 1 tsp | Fresh |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp | Fine sea salt |
| Unsalted Butter | 1 cup | Softened to room temp |
| Granulated Sugar | 3/4 cup | White sugar |
| Brown Sugar | 3/4 cup | Packed light brown |
| Vanilla Extract | 1 tsp | Pure vanilla |
| Large Eggs | 2 | Room temp |
| Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips | 2 cups | 60% cacao preferred |

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions

1. **Preheat & Prepare:** Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. **Mix Dry Ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
3. **Cream Butter & Sugars:** In a large bowl, beat butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla until creamy.
4. **Add Eggs:** Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. **Combine:** Gradually beat in flour mixture until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips.
6. **Bake:** Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto baking sheets. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until golden brown.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips
* For extra chewy cookies, chill the dough in the fridge for at least 1 hour before baking.
* Top with sea salt flakes right after taking them out of the oven!
